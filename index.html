Official Story

Early Life & Origins

Edward Josue Garcia Alcas, professionally known as Garx1z, was born on September 18, 2008, in Coro, Falcon, Venezuela. From an early age, he developed a strong interest in technology, digital creation and music.

His creative interests eventually expanded into music production, digital projects and interactive experiences, allowing him to build a multidisciplinary creative identity around the Garx1z name.

Beginning of His Music Career

Garx1z officially began his musical journey at the age of 17 in 2025. During this period, he started creating and experimenting with his own music, developing his sound independently and learning through each release.

One of his earliest projects was DenimSeals, a project that became an important part of the beginning of his artistic identity. His music was later distributed through major streaming platforms, allowing his work to reach listeners internationally.

Music & Creative Direction

Throughout his development as an artist, Garx1z has experimented with different sounds and styles, including Phonk, Rap, Lofi and alternative urban music.

His catalog reflects an independent approach to music creation, combining vocal experimentation, production, digital aesthetics and influences from different cultures and online music scenes.

x1z RECORDS

As his artistic project developed, Garx1z created x1z RECORDS, an independent creative identity intended to give him greater control over his music and future projects.

Video Game Development & Digital Projects

Beyond music, Garx1z has also explored programming and video game development. His creative projects have included CHAOS RAIN on Roblox and DenimSeals RolePlay, a concept developed for the GTA San Andreas Multiplayer community.

These projects represent another side of his creative work and demonstrate his interest in technology, digital worlds and interactive experiences.

Garx1z Store & Future Projects

Garx1z is also developing an official merchandise presence through the Garx1z Store, with plans centered around clothing, streetwear, collectibles and products connected to his artistic identity.

His long-term vision is to continue developing Garx1z as an independent artist brand, expanding his music catalog, visual identity, digital projects and connection with listeners around the world.
