Garx1z

Artist & Producer

Garx1z (Edward Garcia) is an 18-year-old independent artist and producer based in Lima, Perú. Driven by a lifelong passion for music, his project aims to break the mold and prove that any dream can become reality through persistence.

He draws influence from global legends as well as the raw energy of Pop, Bad Bunny, and Juice WRLD. He loves heavy basslines, hypnotic vocals, and music that makes you move and "feel colors." Inspired by the evolution of rap and the energy of Phonk, Garx1z creates without limits to show that dreams do come true.
