Official Story

The story, music and creative world behind Garx1z.

Garx1z is an independent artist and music producer developing a personal identity through music, visual aesthetics and digital creativity.

His creative journey began with a strong interest in music production and digital culture. Over time, that interest evolved into an independent musical project focused on experimentation and creating a recognizable sound.

Garx1z began releasing music independently in 2025, exploring different sounds and influences while developing his own artistic direction.

His early releases were influenced by Phonk, Rap, Lofi, Trap and Urban music, while his creative direction also incorporates elements inspired by Japanese and Brazilian culture and modern internet aesthetics.

His first single, "DenimSeals", marked an important beginning for the project and helped establish the identity that would continue developing through later releases.

Beyond music, Garx1z has also explored digital creation and game development. These projects form part of his broader creative history and include ideas and projects connected to platforms such as Roblox and GTA San Andreas Multiplayer (SA-MP).

Among these projects are CHAOS RAIN and DenimSeals RolePlay, which reflect his interest in building digital worlds and communities alongside his music.

Garx1z is also building x1z RECORDS as part of his independent creative identity, with the long-term goal of expanding his music, visual branding, merchandise and digital projects.

The project continues to evolve through new releases, collaborations, visual concepts and experiments with different sounds. The objective is to build a recognizable international artist identity while remaining independent and creatively flexible.
