About

Garx1z (Edward Garcia) is an independent artist and music producer based in Lima, Perú. Driven by raw passion and an unrestricted vision, his project blends heavy basslines, hypnotic vocal chops, and dark atmospheric textures across Phonk, Rap, and Lofi soundscapes.

Representing a new era of self-made music production, Garx1z creates without boundaries — turning raw energy into sound that moves fast and leaves a permanent mark.
