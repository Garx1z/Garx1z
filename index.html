About the artist

Garx1z

Music, technology and digital creativity in one project.

Edward Josue Garcia Alcas, professionally known as Garx1z, is an independent Venezuelan artist, music producer and game developer.

Born in Coro, Falcón, Venezuela, Garx1z developed an early interest in computers, digital creation and music production.

His musical work explores different forms of contemporary urban music, including rap, phonk, lo-fi and alternative sounds, while continuing to experiment with new styles and languages.

Alongside music, Garx1z works on interactive and digital projects, including game development and online creative experiences.

He also operates x1z RECORDS, an independent creative label created to maintain control over his music and future projects.
